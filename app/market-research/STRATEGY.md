# Meta tripwire strategy

Care Licensing Solutions. Attractive character: practitioner who owns a licensed ARF and SLS in San Diego and packages the same research for nurses and other healthcare professionals. Voice is the brand in first person. No face required.

## Visual system

The unpaid `/market-research` page borrows long-form structure from pages like Wojo’s Newport Beach letter — hero, founder note, who it’s for, benefits, media, proof, one offer card, guarantee, dark FAQ, final CTA — and keeps the Care Licensing Solutions palette. Bands are black, emerald-950, white, and slate. CTAs, labels, checks, and highlighted phrases are emerald, not amber. It sells the $47 License-First Market Map only. The Launch Kit is not pitched on this page. Checkout, including the $27 checklist bump, and the post-purchase `/upsell` stay on the existing funnel.

## New opportunity

**License-First Market Map.** The deliverable is the existing state + program Market Research Report PDF (50–60 pages). The new opportunity is the order of operations: read the market for one state and one program before a facility, a staff plan, or the full kit.

## Big domino

If a burned-out nurse believes this report shows whether *their* state and program can pay — rates, licensing path, staffing and cost snapshot — they will spend $47 before they spend on a building or the $997 kit.

## False beliefs

- **Vehicle.** Another degree, a consult, or quitting the shift is the way to find out. The vehicle is the map.
- **Internal.** “I know care, not licensing or spreadsheets.” The report is labeled for a clinician, with a how-to-read checklist on the thank-you page.
- **External.** “My state is full / impossible.” The file is the program they pick, or the page says that file is not ready. No generic national PDF.

## Value ladder

Comment or organic “LICENSE” → **$47** report (this page) → **$647** Launch Kit while the $47 credit is open (about $350 off the $997 public kit) → continuity already inside the kit (academy and owner community). Shop report stays $397. Public `/launch-kit` stays $997.

Refunds only for a duplicate charge, access we cannot restore within five business days, or a file we cannot fix within five business days, requested within 14 days. Wrong state or program may be exchanged within 7 days at our discretion. A denied license is not a refund reason.
