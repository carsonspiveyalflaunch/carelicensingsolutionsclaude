# Policy & Procedure Manual Audit — State Bundles

Audit date: 2026-10-14. Scope: P&P manuals in the bundle asset folder (Drive `1_mUC0p_VVkl6MBONXystgMqExso8l5Z3`). Drive was only read; nothing was changed.

## Bottom line

**Rebuild. Don't patch.** Every manual comes from the same automated template: the WeasyPrint "POLICY AND PROCEDURE MANUAL – Version 1.0" layout, also used by the Oklahoma model. The template has problems that show up in every manual:

- Every intro wrongly says the agency must follow **"Medicare Conditions of Participation, 42 CFR Part 484"**. Those are the Medicare rules for home health agencies. They don't apply to RCFEs, ALFs, DD group homes, homemaker/companion agencies or most LHCSAs.
- State rule citations are often **wrong, made up, or point to the wrong program**:
  - TX cites "§553.41(b)–(o)" for 15 policies. §553.41 is "Decrease in Capacity" and has only (a) and (b).
  - MD's only state citation is COMAR 10.21.26.12, a mental-health crisis rule.
  - GA cites only federal ICF/IID rules (42 CFR 483.4xx) and labels them "State". Its policy IDs are literally `GA-ICF-…`.
  - NY cites "765.7(a)–(k)", but Part 765 sections are numbered 765-1.x.
  - FL lists website URLs (chapinc.org, irs.gov, dol.gov) as its "State" citations.
- Procedures are generic boilerplate with the state name swapped in. Every manual says "Oklahoma/Texas/… Form LIC-XXXX".
- Requirements a state surveyor checks first are missing: ratios, training hours, reporting hotlines and timeframes, state forms, and medication-aide rules.
- Some content is unsafe. The NY LHCSA manual has "Direct Care Staff" giving medications. NY aides may not administer medications; only nurses can.
- Buyers get PDFs only (one exception: the CA RCFE also has a Google Doc). That makes the [AGENCY NAME] placeholders hard to fill in.

The Oklahoma 114-page model is the right **length and layout**, but it isn't a quality standard. It has the same 42 CFR 484 claim, "Oklahoma Form LIC-XXXX", and generic procedures, such as background checks "annually" and a governing body meeting "monthly".

## 2. Sampled manuals (6)

Each PDF was downloaded and its full text extracted with pdftotext, so all six were read in full. Every manual has the same structure:
- cover, TOC, copyright and disclaimer, document control
- intro, vision and mission, how to use
- 8 categories of policies, each with a "State: … Federal: …" header, a Policy Statement, Procedures (Responsible / Timing / Documentation), and References
- Appendix A, Required Forms Checklist
- Appendix B, Regulation Crosswalk

Each policy runs about 2–2.7 pages.

### 2.1 TX Assisted Living — 123 pp, 46 policies — Verdict: POOR (citations mostly wrong)
- **(a) State-specific?** In name only. It correctly names HHSC and 26 TAC Ch. 553 and mentions Health & Safety Code Ch. 247. It never mentions Type A vs Type B facilities, Alzheimer's certification, the HHSC Complaint & Incident Intake (CII) line, the Employee Misconduct Registry/NAR check, administrator licensing, or TX medication-aide permits. The med policy has "Pharmacy Staff" doing inventory, but ALFs don't have pharmacy staff.
- **(b) Citations, spot-checked on LII:**
  1. 26 TAC §553.41 is cited for 15 policies with subsections (b)–(o). The real section is "Decrease in Capacity" and has only (a) and (b). **Wrong.**
  2. §553.261 is cited for Resident Trust Funds. It is actually "Coordination of Care". **Wrong.**
  3. §553.259 is cited for the Admission Agreement. It is "Admission Policies and Procedures". **Correct.**
  4. §553.51 is cited for three unrelated policies: Admission Agreement, Billing, and Fire Safety. At least two of those must be wrong.
  5. One reference link in the PDF points to `example.com/26tac55341`, a fake URL.
- **(c) Coverage gaps:** Type A/B evacuation and staffing, Alzheimer's/memory care unit, abuse/neglect/exploitation reporting to HHSC within set timeframes, required admin and attendant training hours, emergency plan per §553.275, resident rights statement form.
- **(d) Depth and format:** Clean layout, [AGENCY NAME]/[Effective Date] placeholders, policy IDs like TX-ALF-xxx. Procedures are generic and lack Texas numbers.
- **(e) Wrong content:** 42 CFR 484 claim; "LIC-XXXX" form placeholder appears 12 times; accreditation standards (CHAP, ACHC) cited as state rules; reference links point to example.com.

### 2.2 CA Assisted Living (RCFE) — 108 pp, 40 policies — Verdict: FAIR (best of the sample; keep as a starting point)
- **(a)** Correct regulator (CDSS Community Care Licensing), correct code (22 CCR Ch. 8, HSC 1569.x), mentions LIC 602.
- **(b) Spot-checks on LII:**
  - 87215 "Commingling of Money", cited for resident funds: correct.
  - 87467 "Resident Participation in Decisionmaking", cited for care planning: reasonable.
  - 87223 "Relocation of Resident", cited for facility closure: partly right. Closure also needs HSC 1569.682.
  - 87508 "Register of Residents", cited for records: partly right.
  - HSC 1569.269 cited for resident rights: correct.
  - Overall: mostly real and roughly on topic. Some "State" citations are CHAP/ACHC standards.
- **(c) Gaps:** LTC Ombudsman posting/notification, the 80-hour administrator certification and 40-hour continuing education, direct care staff training hours (40 h before working alone + 20 h/yr), LIC 601/603/9172 forms, the 3-day notice for evictions, and the new 87468.1/.2 rights structure.
- **(e)** 42 CFR 484 claim (RCFEs are not Medicare-certified); LIC-XXXX appears 11 times.

### 2.3 MD Residential Care DD (Residential Habilitation) — 131 pp, 49 policies — Verdict: FAIL
- **(a)** Generic. It names "MDH" and mentions DDA only six times. It never cites COMAR 10.22 (the DDA rules). It doesn't mention OHCQ licensing, Certified Medication Technicians and delegating nurses (COMAR 10.27.11), Coordinators of Community Services, LTSSMaryland, or DDA's Policy on Reportable Incidents. There is **no behavior support or restraint policy**, even though this is a DD residential program.
- **(b) Spot-checks:**
  - The only state rule cited, COMAR 10.21.26.12, is "Transition and Discharge" for *Community Mental Health Residential Crisis Services*. **Wrong program.**
  - 42 CFR 441.301(c)(4)(vi), cited for Medication Management, is actually the HCBS rule for provider-owned residential settings (leases, privacy, visitors). **Wrong.**
  - Other 441.301 citations are real HCBS rules but are labeled as "State" citations.
  - Three policies say "State: N/A Federal: N/A".
- **(e)** 42 CFR 484 claim.

### 2.4 FL Personal Home Care (no FL PHC manual exists; read Homemaker_companion_agencies.pdf instead) — 64 pp, 21 policies — Verdict: FAIL
- **(a)** The intro falsely says "there is no specific state regulation governing homemaker companion agencies." In fact, AHCA registers them under s. 400.509 F.S., and they need Level 2 screening under ch. 435 / s. 408.809. The manual cites 400.506 and 400.484 once each; those are nurse-registry and home-health penalty provisions.
- **(b)** No state rule is cited in any policy header. "State" fields are URLs such as chapinc.org/standards (9 times), achc.org (4), irs.gov and dol.gov. **Fails every spot-check.**
- **(c)** Missing: AHCA registration and renewal, Level 2 screening and the AHCA Clearinghouse, the FL Abuse Hotline (1-800-96-ABUSE) mandatory reporting, and the companion vs. personal care scope limit. ADRD training requirements still need to be checked.
- **(d)** Thinnest manual: 21 policies and half the length of the OK model.
- **(e)** 42 CFR 484 is cited 18 times; LIC-XXXX appears 5 times.

### 2.5 NY Personal Home Care (LHCSA) — 123 pp, 46 policies — Verdict: POOR (unsafe in places)
- **(a)** Correct regulator (NYSDOH) and mentions the Home Care Worker Registry, CHRC and PHL 3605. Doesn't mention Wage Parity (PHL 3614-c), EVV, the Justice Center, nurse supervisory visits for aides, or the LHCSA need methodology and moratorium history.
- **(b) Spot-checks:**
  - "10 NYCRR 765.7(a)–(k)", "765.10", "765.11", "765.13" are cited about 80 times. Part 765 is "Approval and Licensure" and its sections are numbered 765-1.x / 765-2.x, so **these section numbers don't exist.** The operating standards are in Part 766 (766.11, personnel, is cited correctly once).
  - PHL §3613 is cited for criminal background checks, but it covers the Home Care Worker Registry. CHRC is PHL §2899-a.
  - One header reads "Federal: 42 CFR 484.X", an unfilled placeholder.
- **(e) Unsafe:** The med policy says "Direct Care Staff shall administer medications". NY aides may only assist with self-administration; administration is nursing practice. It also cites a "New York Form for Medication Administration" that doesn't exist.

### 2.6 GA Residential Care DD — 142 pp, 54 policies — Verdict: FAIL (wrong program)
- **(a)** Names DBHDD as the regulator. The license for DD group homes is actually a Community Living Arrangement (CLA) license from DCH Healthcare Facility Regulation, Ga. Comp. R. & Regs. 111-8-1 (verify chapter number). DBHDD only enrolls providers under its Provider Manual. The manual cites **no Georgia rule at all**: 42 CFR 483 (ICF/IID) appears 184 times, labeled as "State". Policy IDs are `GA-ICF-…`.
- **(b)** 42 CFR 483.420(a), .440, .460 and .470 are real ICF/IID rules, but they don't apply to a CLA or waiver group home. **Wrong framework.**
- **(c)** Missing: CLA licensing, DBHDD Provider Manual standards, ISP/support coordination, NOW/COMP waiver, DBHDD critical incident reporting, and HRST.
- **(e)** 42 CFR 484 claim; leftover placeholders LIC-XXX/XXXX.

## 3. Comparison with the Oklahoma 114-page model
- **Same generator.** Same cover, document control, "Introduction / Vision / How to Use" sections, 8 policy categories, per-policy State/Federal header, Policy Statement / Procedures / References, Appendix A Forms Checklist and Appendix B Crosswalk. OK has 41 policies (~26k words), the bundle manuals 21–54 (14–34k words). In length and structure the bundle manuals already match or exceed the model, except FL Homemaker (64 pp) and a few small files (CA HCO ≈55 pp, PA CRF ≈57 pp, VA HCO ≈60 pp).
- **The model has the same problems:** it says 42 CFR 484 applies to a residential habilitation center, uses an "Oklahoma Form LIC-XXXX" placeholder, has generic roles (IT Manager, Policy Review Committee) and unsupported frequencies (annual background re-checks, monthly governing body meetings). Its citations (OAC 340:100, 42 CFR 483.4xx) are more relevant to its program than GA's or MD's are to theirs.
- **Use the OK model as a layout and length target, not a content standard.** A rebuilt manual should keep the shell and sections. It should add: real state rule mapping for each requirement, state forms by number, state numbers (ratios, training hours, reporting deadlines, hotlines), and program-correct policy sets. For DD programs that includes behavior support/restraint, incident reporting to the state system, and medication technician/delegation rules.

## 4. Most common problems (all 6 samples, plus the OK model)
1. Wrong federal framework: "Medicare CoPs 42 CFR Part 484" in every intro (7/7).
2. State citations are made up, mis-numbered, or for another program (TX, NY, MD, GA, FL). Only CA is mostly accurate.
3. Federal rules (HIPAA 45 CFR 164, OSHA 1910.1030, ICF 483) and accreditation standards (CHAP/ACHC) filled in as "State" citations.
4. Leftover generator placeholders: "LIC-XXXX" forms, "42 CFR 484.X", example.com links.
5. Generic procedures with no state numbers: ratios, training hours, reporting timeframes, hotlines, state forms.
6. Program-critical policies missing: DD behavior support/restraint (MD), medication aide/CMT rules (TX, MD), Ombudsman (CA), registration and screening (FL).
7. Unsafe instructions in some places (NY aides administering medications).
8. Program mismatches in the inventory: GA DD is an ICF manual, VA DD is a CSB manual, NJ and NC have no group-home manual. FL and PA home care manuals were never generated.
9. PDF-only delivery means buyers can't easily customize the [AGENCY NAME] placeholders.

## 5. Recommendation: rebuild with a corrected pipeline; reuse the shell
- **Why not keep and correct:** The errors are in the template, not one-off typos. Every manual needs its citations, intro, procedures and policy list rewritten. That is a rebuild done by hand, page by page, across about 36 manuals, with nothing guaranteeing the result.
- **What to reuse:** The layout and section order (it matches the OK model's 100+ page standard) and the Appendix A/B ideas. CA RCFE can be a starting draft after a citation pass.
- **What the rebuild needs:**
  1. A per-state, per-program requirements source (rule chapter, section map, forms, numbers), checked against the official code before writing.
  2. Policy sets chosen per program, not one shared list.
  3. Remove the 42 CFR 484 claim. Cite federal rules only where they really apply: HCBS Settings 441.301 for waiver programs, ICF 483.400+ only for ICF manuals, HIPAA, OSHA.
  4. An automated check that rejects "LIC-XXXX", ".X", example.com, and cited sections that don't exist.
  5. Deliver an editable Google Doc/DOCX alongside the PDF.
- **Priority order:** Pull or fix the wrong-program files first (GA DD, VA DD). Next, fill the gaps (FL and PA home care, NJ and NC DD). Then rebuild the TX, NY and MD manuals. CA RCFE comes last (citation cleanup only).
